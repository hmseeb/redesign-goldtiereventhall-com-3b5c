The Venue

A blank canvas that already looks finished

Gold Tier Event Hall was designed so you spend less time sourcing rentals and more time enjoying your event. Our main ballroom pairs 20-foot ceilings and warm architectural lighting with a neutral, modern palette that works with any color story — from black-tie gold to soft coastal white.

The room divides into two independent spaces, so a 60-guest rehearsal dinner feels just as intentional as a 250-guest reception. A private bridal suite, dedicated catering prep kitchen, built-in bar and covered entry for rainy-day arrivals are all part of the hall — not add-ons.

  • 6,000 sq. ft. column-free ballroom with retractable divider
  • Private bridal / green room with vanity and lounge seating
  • Commercial prep kitchen open to your licensed caterer
  • Built-in bar, dance floor and staged head-table area
  • Free on-site parking with accessible entry and restrooms
